Premium automotive editorial photograph for RotaGuard, a Brazilian driver vigilance hardware concept. A pristine, unbranded pearl-white modern Brazilian high-deck long-distance intercity touring coach drives toward the camera on a gently curving mountain highway. Lush deep forest-green Atlantic forest foothills, distant misty mountains, realistic asphalt and safety barrier. Elevated 85mm three-quarter frontal camera, complete coach in the right third and lower half. The left third is darker forest with room for white website typography added later. ## 2. Caixa conceitual — protagonista do produto

Objetivo: tornar a proposta física reconhecível. Sem aparência de servidor, webcam de mesa ou monitor. Fundo claro e sombra natural, usados com tratamento CSS de mistura de cor.

```text
Photorealistic industrial product visualization of a tiny RotaGuard driver-monitoring edge device. One closed rectangular graphite anodized-aluminum housing, approximately 110mm wide, 72mm deep and 40mm tall, thin cooling fins along top and sides, gently rounded corners. Black front optical glass with one centered camera lens, four tiny infrared emitter dots and one pale green status indicator. Discreet RotaGuard wordmark, no screen or antenna. Low-profile removable black mounting cradle. Slightly elevated three-quarter front 70mm camera; entire product centered, filling approximately 72 percent of the frame. Pale off-white green seamless studio surface, soft credible contact shadow, large upper-left softbox and controlled graphite highlights. Rich black glass and realistic machined texture. No props, decorative typography, other brands or website interface. This is a concept design visualization, not a manufactured-device photograph. Landscape 3:2.
```

Aceite: caixa completa, lente legível, consistência do material. A inscrição RotaGuard na imagem foi gerada; a marca vetorial do site é independente.
